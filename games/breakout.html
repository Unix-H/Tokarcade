Breakout

Move the paddle with your mouse, finger or arrow keys. Click or press space to launch.

How to play

  • Mouse / touch or ←→ to move
  • Space launch the ball / pause
  • Hit the ball with the paddle’s edge to change its angle. Higher rows score more. You have three lives.
